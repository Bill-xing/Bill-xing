## About

I am pursuing a second bachelor's degree in **Computer Science and Technology** at **Harbin Institute of Technology, Shenzhen**. I received my B.Eng. in **Mechanical Design, Manufacturing and Automation** from **Harbin Institute of Technology, Weihai**.

My current focus is **embodied AI**, especially **Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models for real-robot manipulation**. I am interested in building physically grounded intelligent systems that connect perception, action, learning, and real-world robotic execution.

With a background across mechanical engineering and computer science, I am also working on robotics software systems that bridge learning-based methods and real-machine constraints.
